Method

Where the ideas come from.

I have lived in Mogadishu, Nairobi, Dhaka and Chittagong. Comparing those places shows me what is missing at home. My training is in accounting, finance and banking, so every idea gets priced before it gets built.

  1. Spot the gap

    Find a service people here need and cannot get, or get only at a poor price.

  2. Design for local customers

    Change the product, price and payment method to fit local habits, in US dollars and mobile money.

  3. Prove it small, then fund the next one

    Test with a handful of customers by hand. Running businesses pay for the next experiment.
