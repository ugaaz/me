About

I build what the market is missing.

I'm Ugaaz Arafat, an entrepreneur based in Mogadishu and Nairobi. I spot what Somali markets are missing and build businesses that fit how people here live, buy and pay.

Before founding my own companies, I worked in accounting at Mogadishu businesses including Tamarso Solar Energy, Habeb Mental Hospitals and Hayat Market. That taught me how businesses here actually run: cash, suppliers, and customers who pay by mobile money.

I hold an MBA, a BBA, a B.Sc. in Computer Science from India, and a Post-Graduate Diploma in Banking and Finance.

Ventures

Businesses I've built, and what's next

  1. Bagaash

    Live

    bagaash.com · Founder · Since July 2025

    Monthly grocery bundles delivered to Mogadishu families. Customers order on WhatsApp and pay with EVC Plus, eDahab or Waafi.

  2. Bogso

    Live

    bogso.so · CEO

    Home healthcare in Mogadishu. Nurses visit patients at home for basic care, wound treatment and sample collection, and a doctor reviews results remotely. Care costs much less than a hospital visit, and the model creates paid work for nursing and medical graduates. "Bogso" means "get well" in Somali.

  3. Bajaj ride app

    Validating

    Mogadishu · In research

    A platform that connects riders with the nearest available Bajaj, with shared rides for two passengers. Taxi apps already exist here; Bajaj passengers still have no platform.
