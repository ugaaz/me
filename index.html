About

A voice for evidence in a country living the climate crisis.

I am Aziiz Guuleed, an environmental scientist, researcher and senior lecturer. My work sits at the intersection of environmental science, climate change, governance, sustainable development and public policy, with particular attention to Somalia and the wider Horn of Africa.

I am interested in environmental research, climate science, water quality, pollution, ecosystem degradation, GIS and remote sensing, and the use of environmental data to understand changing landscapes, communities and development patterns.
