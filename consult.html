Consultation

Think with the evidence, not around it.

Aziiz Guuleed advises governments, universities, NGOs, journalists and student groups that need environmental science translated into decisions for Somalia and the Horn of Africa.

How it works

A short path from question to conversation.

No fake calendar. You describe the problem; Guuleed Green replies with whether a scoping call, a briefing or a longer advisory fit is right.

01

Request

Tell us who you are, the environmental question, and when you need a first conversation.

02

Scope

A short reply confirms fit, format and whether the work is a call, a written note, a lecture or a longer engagement.

03

Work

The consultation itself: evidence, maps, policy language and teaching — aimed at something you can actually use.

Formats

What a consultation can be.

Built around the same eight focus areas as the rest of the site — governance, climate, pollution, water, cities, GIS, communication and sustainable development.

Policy

Policy & governance briefing

For ministries, local authorities and programmes that need a clear reading of environmental rules, mandates and what is missing on the ground.

Research

Research & data advisory

Study design, water and pollution questions, GIS and remote sensing — turning measurements into an argument a decision-maker can hold.

Teaching

Lectures, workshops & climate communication

Guest teaching, public talks and help turning technical findings into language for students, press and citizens.

Place

Somalia & the Horn, in context

Advisory that treats drought, displacement, cities and rangelands as one system — not as four separate donor themes.

Who this is for

Public institutions, universities, NGOs, journalists, researchers and serious student groups. Independent support is separate — use the Support page to give.

  • Typical first step A 45-minute scoping conversation, remote or in person where possible.
  • Then, if it fits A briefing note, lecture, data review or a defined advisory period.
  • Email hello@guuledgreen.com
  • Not a booking widget Availability is confirmed by reply so the work is not reduced to an empty calendar slot.
