What support funds

Guuleed Green is an independent platform. Contributions keep the research, writing and teaching public — not locked behind a consultancy invoice.

  • Research & analysis Briefings on governance, climate, water, waste and urban environment.
  • Data-driven projects GIS, mapping and environmental datasets that make change visible.
  • Public ideas Essays, lectures and teaching materials for a wider Somali audience.
  • How to give EVC Plus, Zaad, bank transfer or international transfer — details arranged after you write.
